Privacy Policy

Clipper runs only on its owner's computer and is used only by its owner.

What it uses

When the owner connects a TikTok or Instagram account, Clipper receives sign-in tokens, the account's username and id, and, later, view counts for the videos it posted. It uses them only to post the owner's clips to the owner's own accounts and to show how those clips perform.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Tokens are kept in a local settings file. Nothing is sold, shared or sent anywhere except to TikTok and Instagram themselves, when posting or reading view counts.

This page

This website only shows the sign-in code back to the owner after they approve Clipper. It stores nothing and has no analytics or cookies.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect an account in Clipper at any time, or remove Clipper's access in the TikTok or Instagram app settings.
